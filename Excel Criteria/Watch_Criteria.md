# Watch Criteria — WPM

> **Important:** We can POTENTIALLY still run a case if anything falls outside of this criteria, BUT **SPEAK WITH TOM OR DEBRA BEFORE BOOKING THE CASE IN.**
>
> Will want evidence of any vulnerabilities being used to be outside of the below criteria.

---

## Rejection Rules

| Condition | Action |
|-----------|--------|
| Debt can be paid off in less than 6 years | **Reject IVA** |
| Client only has debt with 1 lender (need separate lender account of more than £500) | **Reject IVA** |
| Client has children over 13 and no sustainability paragraph (Drafter) | **Reject** |
| Client aged 80 or above | **Abstain (not vote)** |
| Antecedent transactions appear — irrelevant of the dividend factor | **Reject all cases** |
| Car finance taken in last 3 months, without evidence of valid reason (e.g. old car scrapped with proof) | **Reject** |

---

## Modification Rules

| Condition | Action |
|-----------|--------|
| Car value is more than £9,000 and funds brought into IVA | Request downgrade to £4,500 |
| Car HP is > £400 per month (unless family help or valid reason evidenced) | **Modify** |

---

## Additional Requirements

- **Gambling as main cause of debt:** Will require 3 months clean bank statement.
- **Previously proposed IVA:** The main content (I&E, assets, liabilities) needs to remain the same, or a reason must be provided for the changes.
